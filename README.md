# FocusFlow - Roam Less. Discover More.

Transform your browsing into a productive journey with FocusFlow - your digital companion that helps you browse with purpose.

##  What is FocusFlow?

FocusFlow is a Chrome extension that gamifies your browsing experience to help you build better digital habits. Watch your attention monster grow through mastery stages as you maintain focus, complete daily goals, and browse with intention.

## ✨ Key Features

###  **Smart Analytics Dashboard**
- Track your daily focus time and browsing patterns
- Visualize your productivity with beautiful charts
- Monitor your focus quality (Deep, Active, Light)
- See which sites help you stay productive

###  **Daily Goals System**
- Set and track meaningful objectives
- Get AI-powered goal suggestions based on your browsing
- Celebrate achievements with satisfying animations
- Build streaks to maintain momentum

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Insights**
- Get intelligent summaries of articles you read
- Receive personalized productivity recommendations
- Ask questions about your browsing patterns
- Discover insights about your digital habits

### 🐾 **Progress Companion**
- Your digital companion evolves through mastery stages
- Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Master
- Complete goals to advance stages
- Track progress, energy, and knowledge stats

###  **Knowledge Network**
- Automatically build a visual map of what you learn
- Connect concepts across different articles
- Search through your reading history intelligently
- Never lose track of important information

### 🏆 **Focus Points & Levels**
- Earn points for maintaining focus
- Level up as you browse productively
- Unlock new mastery stages
- Track your progress with visual indicators

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/focusflow.git
   ```

2. **Load in Chrome:**
   - Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
   - Enable "Developer mode" (top right)
   - Click "Load unpacked"
   - Select the FocusFlow folder

3. **Start browsing!**
   - Click the FocusFlow icon in your toolbar
   - Watch your attention monster and start building better habits

##  How It Works

### Focus States
FocusFlow tracks three types of focus:

- **🔵 Deep Focus** (10+ minutes on a page) - Maximum productivity
- **🟢 Active Focus** (5-10 minutes) - Engaged reading
- **⚪ Light Browsing** (< 5 minutes) - Quick exploration

### Mastery Stages
your attention monster evolves as you complete goals:

1. **Foundation** (Start) - Begin your journey
2. **Beginner** (1+ goals) - Building habits
3. **Intermediate** (6+ goals) - Developing consistency
4. **Master** (16+ goals) - Peak performance

### Daily Goals
- Add meaningful objectives for your day
- Break them down into actionable tasks
- Complete goals to boost your attention monster's progress
- Build streaks for consistent productivity

## 🎮 Tips for Success

1. **Set Clear Goals**: Start each day by setting 3-5 achievable goals
2. **Focus Deeply**: Aim for at least 2 hours of deep focus daily
3. **Review Analytics**: Check your dashboard weekly to spot patterns
4. **Build Streaks**: Consistency is key to forming habits
5. **Engage with Content**: The AI learns from your focused reading

## 🛠️ Technical Details

### Built With
- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- **AI Features**: Chrome's built-in AI APIs
- **Data Visualization**: Chart.js, Vis.js
- **Storage**: Chrome Storage API
- **Architecture**: Event-driven background service worker

### Browser Requirements
- Chrome 127 or higher
- AI features require Chrome's experimental AI APIs

### Privacy First
- All data stays local on your device
- No external servers or tracking
- You own your browsing data
- Export/import functionality for backup
