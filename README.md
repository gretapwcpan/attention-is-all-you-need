# AttenGame - Roam Less. Discover More.

Transform your browsing into a productive journey with AttenGame - your digital companion that helps you browse with purpose.

##  What is AttenGame?

AttenGame is a Chrome extension that gamifies your browsing experience to help you build better digital habits. Watch your attention monster grow through mastery stages as you maintain focus, complete daily goals, and browse with intention.

###  **Smart Analytics Dashboard**
- Track your daily focus time and browsing patterns

- Monitor your focus quality (Deep, Active, Light)
- See which sites help you stay productive

###  **Daily Goals System**
- Set and track meaningful objectives
- Get AI-powered goal suggestions based on your browsing
- Celebrate achievements with satisfying animations
- Build streaks to maintain momentum

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Insights**
- Get intelligent summaries of articles you read
- Receive personalized productivity recommendations
- Ask questions about your browsing patterns
- Discover insights about your digital habits

### 🐾 **Progress Companion**
- Your digital companion evolves through mastery stages
- Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Master
- Complete goals to advance stages
- Track progress, energy, and knowledge stats

###  **Knowledge Network**
- Automatically build a visual map of what you learn
- Connect concepts across different articles
- Search through your reading history intelligently
- Never lose track of important information

### 🏆 **Focus Points & Levels**
- Earn points for maintaining focus
- Level up as you browse productively
- Unlock new mastery stages
- Track your progress with visual indicators

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 📥 Download the Extension

```bash
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/AttenGame.git
cd AttenGame
```

### 🔧 Install in Chrome

1. **Open Chrome Extension Manager:**
   - Open Google Chrome
   - Type `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar and press Enter
   - Or: Click the three dots menu (⋮) → More tools → Extensions

2. **Enable Developer Mode:**
   - Look for the **"Developer mode"** toggle in the top-right corner
   - Click to turn it ON (the toggle should be blue)

3. **Load the Extension:**
   - Click the **"Load unpacked"** button that appears
   - Navigate to the folder where you extracted/cloned AttenGame
   - Select the entire **AttenGame** folder (not individual files)
   - Click **"Select Folder"** (Windows) or **"Select"** (Mac)

4. **Verify Installation:**
   - You should see AttenGame appear in your extensions list
   - Look for the AttenGame icon in your Chrome toolbar (puzzle piece area)
   - If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece icon and pin AttenGame
5. **Enable Gemini Nano API by Following Docs:**
   - refer to the doc to load the local model to your chrome browser https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/prompt-api

### 🎯 First Time Setup

1. **Click the AttenGame icon** in your toolbar to open the popup
2. **Your attention companion** will start at the Foundation stage
3. **Set your first daily goal** to begin your productivity journey
4. **Browse normally** - AttenGame will track your focus automatically
5. **Check your progress** anytime by clicking the extension icon

### ❓ Troubleshooting

**Extension not loading?**
- Make sure you selected the main AttenGame folder (containing `manifest.json`)
- Check that Developer Mode is enabled
- Try refreshing the extensions page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)

**Icon not visible?**
- Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome toolbar
- Find AttenGame and click the pin icon to keep it visible

**"Manifest file missing" error?**
- Ensure you're selecting the correct folder
- The folder must contain the `manifest.json` file
- If you downloaded as ZIP, make sure it's fully extracted

**Chrome version too old?**
- AttenGame requires Chrome 127 or newer
- Update Chrome: Settings → About Chrome → Update if available

### 🔄 Updating the Extension

When updates are available:
1. Download or pull the latest version
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/`
3. Find AttenGame and click the refresh icon (🔄)
4. Or click "Remove" and repeat the installation steps

##  How It Works

### Focus States
AttenGame tracks three types of focus:

- **🔵 Deep Focus** (10+ minutes on a page) - Maximum productivity
- **🟢 Active Focus** (5-10 minutes) - Engaged reading
- **⚪ Light Browsing** (< 5 minutes) - Quick exploration

### Mastery Stages
your attention monster evolves as you complete goals:

1. **Foundation** (Start) - Begin your journey
2. **Beginner** (1+ goals) - Building habits
3. **Intermediate** (6+ goals) - Developing consistency
4. **Master** (16+ goals) - Peak performance

### Daily Goals
- Add meaningful objectives for your day
- Break them down into actionable tasks
- Complete goals to boost your attention monster's progress
- Build streaks for consistent productivity

## 🎮 Tips for Success

1. **Set Clear Goals**: Start each day by setting 3-5 achievable goals
2. **Focus Deeply**: Aim for at least 2 hours of deep focus daily
3. **Review Analytics**: Check your dashboard weekly to spot patterns
4. **Build Streaks**: Consistency is key to forming habits
5. **Engage with Content**: The AI learns from your focused reading
